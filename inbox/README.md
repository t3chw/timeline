# Inbox

Drop anything here that you want turned into notes: text files, Markdown, screenshots, PDFs, rough
jottings. Then tell Claude to **update**.

Claude will turn the material into dated notes in `src/content/entries/`, copy any images or PDFs the
notes need into `public/files/`, and move the originals into `inbox/_processed/`. Nothing is deleted.

You don't have to use the inbox. Telling Claude what you learned in chat works just as well.
